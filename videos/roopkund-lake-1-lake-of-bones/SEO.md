## Deliberately excluded — don't add these without reconsidering why

- **"roopkund lake mystery dhruv rathee"** — autocomplete suggests real search volume, but it
  targets a specific named creator's audience rather than describing our own content. Gray-hat
  SEO, not a hard rule violation, but a deliberate choice to skip — revisit only if the channel
  decides that trade-off is worth it.
- **"...in hindi" / "...in telugu" / "...in tamil" / "...in malayalam"** — real demand, but our
  videos are English-language. Tagging a language the video isn't in is metadata
  misrepresentation and risks a penalty. Only add a language tag to a version actually produced
  in that language (e.g. a future dubbed cut).
- **"roopkund vlogger" / "roopkund trek budget"** — travel/trek-planning search intent, a
  different audience than true-mystery viewers. Would add views but tank click-through
  relevance and average view duration.
