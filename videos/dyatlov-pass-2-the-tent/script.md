Format: 1080×1920 @ 30fps, ~31s. Hybrid (`/make-video`): TSX stat cards + AI atmosphere

## Facts (verified)

Sometime after nightfall on Feb 1, 1959, the nine hikers cut their way OUT of their own
tent from the inside, then fled downhill into open snow — inadequately dressed (some in
socks, one barefoot), in wind and cold reaching an estimated −25 to −40°C.
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyatlov_Pass_incident).

Editorial rule carries over from part 1: no AI-generated faces/likenesses of the real
victims — fleeing figures, if shown at all, are distant silhouettes, never depicted faces.
