## Facts (verified before scripting)

- **Three rival theories existed before the DNA study**, per the 2019 Harney et al. paper's own
  background section: (1) the local-legend pilgrimage (a king, queen, and retinue struck down
  by the goddess Nanda Devi — already told in Part 2), (2) "an army or group of merchants who
  were caught in a storm," and (3) "victims of an epidemic." [Harney et al. 2019, full text —
  PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6702210/) Parts 1–2 already ruled out the
  army/weapons theory on forensic grounds (no sword or arrow wounds, injuries confined to
  head/shoulders) — this part's beat 2 states that rejection explicitly and adds the epidemic
  theory as a second rival the wound pattern also argues against (a blunt, round, head-only
  injury is not how disease kills), without inventing new forensic detail beyond what Parts 1–2
  already established.
- **The 2004 physical-anthropology report**: Subhash Walimbe (Deccan College, Pune) prepared
  the report "used as the basis of a National Geographic documentary" (explicitly credited in
  the Harney et al. 2019 paper's acknowledgments). That report identified "both very robust and
  tall individuals (outside the range of almost all South Asians), and more gracile
  individuals," hypothesizing "the presence of at least two distinct groups." [Harney et al.
  2019, full text — PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6702210/) This is the real,
  citable, PRE-DNA-study finding this part's beats 5–6 dramatize — rendered in plain language
  ("robust and tall" vs. "slighter, smaller") rather than the technical term "gracile," and
  framed only as two body-type groups, never as genetic ancestry or a timeline claim (that's
  Part 4's DNA reveal, not this one).
- **The "porters" reading of the smaller/shorter group** is the natural, commonly reported
  interpretation (pilgrims typically travel with local hired guides on Himalayan routes) and is
  presented here as the leading theory's own explanation, not as a separately sourced fact —
  phrased with "looked like," not stated as confirmed identity.
- **The Nanda Devi Raj Jat pilgrimage is a real, still-practiced tradition**, held roughly once
  every twelve years, passing near Roopkund on the way to the Homkund shrine — carried over
  from Part 1/2's own sourcing. [Wikipedia — Nanda Devi Raj
  Jat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanda_Devi_Raj_Jat)
- **What this part does NOT say** (reserved for Part 4, per Part 2's own script.md/beats.json
  notes): no mention of DNA, genetic ancestry, "three groups," "Mediterranean," or "1,000 years
  apart." This part's leading theory is stated as the accepted answer "for decades" — a framing
  that is itself the setup for Part 4's overturning of it, not a claim that it's still the final
  word.

**Editorial rule (all 5 parts, same as Part 1, Part 2, and Dyatlov Pass):** no AI-generated
likeness claimed as a real, documented person. The 2004 forensic report and Subhash Walimbe are
referenced in VO only, never depicted (beat 4 shows hands/trays/specimens only, no face). The
"porters"/guides in beat 6 and the pilgrims implied by the reused legend still (beat 3) are
generic, distant, or back-turned figures — a theorized identity, not a documented portrait.

## Production notes

- Engine mix: `image` (Ken Burns, `tools/gen_image.py`, `fast` preset / Nano Banana 2 — ~$0.04
  each) for atmosphere/forensic/route beats, `tsx` for the two-groups diagram + title-card
  beats — see `beats.json`'s `beats[].engine`.
- **6 new stills needed** (hook, rival-theories, forensic-return, porters, real-route,
  leading-theory) ≈ **$0.24 total** at the `fast` preset — state this before generating,
  per repo convention. Beats 3 and 7 are reuses (Part 2's legend still, Part 1's wound still) —
  no new generation cost for those two.
- New TSX component `RoopkundTwoGroupsChart` (beat 5) added to `lib/roopkund.tsx` — an abstract
  height-comparison diagram (two silhouette groups), same dark/frost-blue visual language as
  `RoopkundTrapDiagram`, no figures with faces, so no editorial-rule risk.
- Beat 3 reuses Part 2's `02-legend.png` still (no second generation) — a deliberate visual
  callback tying this part's "who were they" question back to the same legend imagery, distinct
  from the wound-still throughline (which is its own separate recurring motif, reused again at
  beat 7 per the series' own stated convention).
- Beat 10's title card reuses THIS episode's own beat-1 hook still (not Part 1's or Part 2's) —
  same pattern as every prior part's loop-closing beat.
- No engagement-CTA outro. "Find out in Part 4" is the series' own cliffhanger structure — VO
  ends on the payoff line, title card fades out before the loop point.
- Cliffhanger deliberately does NOT preview any DNA/genetics/timeline specifics — it only
  signals that the "pilgrims + guides, one storm" answer will be tested, setting up Part 4's
  reveal without leaking it.

  "google-flow-manual"` in each still's sidecar `.json` under `media/projects/`) — no Flow
  credit count was recorded. `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` was present and worked normally for voice.

  OVERFLOW (real speech needed more room than the ~2.7wps estimate even at the 1.3x tempo cap).
  Every beat from `forensic-return` onward was pushed later per the repo's VO retiming
  procedure (push the gap, don't `--force` re-bill); `format.durationSec` extended 60.5 → 61.5.
  Final `beats.json` and `remotion/src/shots/roopkund-lake-3/vo.gen.ts` carry the real
  ElevenLabs alignment. Full composition rendered, voice muxed, and both frame-by-frame QA
  and the voiced preview were checked — awaiting the user's ear before SFX/music.
